# COMMUNITY_NOT_IN_CLUB — The connected club does not belong to this community.

## Meaning

The connected club does not belong to this community.

## Typical causes

- A community's pages can only be edited through one of its member clubs, and the connected club is not one of them.
- The club has left the community in the meantime.
- The community identifier belongs to a different community.

## Solution

1. Sign in with a member club of the community: `comvenio login`
2. Without a member club at hand, edit the community page in the web app.
