# NOT_FOUND — The requested entry was not found.

## Meaning

The requested entry was not found.

## Typical causes

- The identifier does not belong to an entry visible in the connected club.
- The entry has since been removed.
- The wrong club is connected.

## Solution

1. Check the identifier against the matching list: `comvenio action list`
2. If in doubt about the connected club, sign in again: `comvenio login`
